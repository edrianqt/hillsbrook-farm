Hillsbrook Farm began on **27 May 2015**, when Edelito Caindoy Sangco started farming on the hills of Sitio Carbon, Barangay Del Pilar, in Socorro. The name says what the place is: a farm on the hills, traversed by a brook.

Edelito studied Animal Husbandry & Dairying at SNSU–Mainit, and dairy was always part of the plan. [details: how the first cows came to the farm, and when the first milk was sold]

[photo: the farm in its early years]

Today Hillsbrook is a family-owned dairy farm with a crossbred Holstein Friesian herd of five cows and four calves. We grow our own Napier grass and corn for feed, milk in our own Milking Parlor, and sell **Island Pure Cow's Milk** across the water in Siargao.

The farm is also an ATI-accredited Learning Site on Diversified Farming and a TESDA-accredited Farm School, so our gate is open to students, visitors and fellow farmers.
