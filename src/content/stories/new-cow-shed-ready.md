Our new cow shed is finished, and it is ready for **five pregnant Holstein Friesian heifers** from the National Dairy Authority (NDA).

The shed was funded through the **Coconut Farmers and Industry Development Plan (CFIDP)** via the Philippine Coconut Authority (PCA). We built it with the cows' comfort first:

- **automatic water troughs**, so the cows can drink whenever they want
- **anti-slip floors**, so they stand and walk safely, even when the floor is wet
- **roof louvers** that let hot air out and keep the shed cool in the island heat
- a **loafing area** where the cows can rest and move freely

<img src="images/optimized/farm__herd-in-cow-shed-798.webp" alt="Holstein cows feeding at the trough inside the new shed, with two farmers" width="798" height="597" loading="lazy">

Comfortable cows are healthy cows, and healthy cows give good milk. Thank you to the NDA, the CFIDP and the PCA for helping a small island farm grow.
