In March 2019 we welcomed some of our first farm tourists from far away: **three guests from Argentina and one from Paraguay**.

They walked the farm, met the animals and saw how we work on the hills of Bucas Grande Island.

On **28 March 2019**, one of our guests from Argentina planted a **Tabebuia rosea**, the pink trumpet tree, on the farm as a gesture of friendship. Every time it flowers, we remember that visit.

Planting a tree with our guests has become one of our favourite ways to say thank you. If you visit, ask us about it.
