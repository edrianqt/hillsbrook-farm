It was a proud moment for Hillsbrook Farm. Our audio-visual presentation was featured and warmly applauded during the **2-Day Dairy Farmers' Forum** organized by the National Dairy Authority–Northern Mindanao, on **11–12 August 2026** at Chali Beach Resort and Conference Center in Cagayan de Oro City.

The forum brought together dairy farmers and partners for the Annual Dairy Farmers' Consultative Forum and the Dairy Farmers' Business Forum: two days of discussion on how the industry can grow, the challenges farmers face, and the opportunities ahead.

We shared the Hillsbrook story: our journey as a family-owned dairy cattle farm on Bucas Grande Island, our crossbred Holstein Friesian herd, our sustainable practices, and our growing work in fresh milk and value-added products.
