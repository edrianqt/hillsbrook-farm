Some of the best days on the farm have nothing to do with work.

On a Sunday in September, two of our grandchildren arrived at Hillsbrook with one plan: to visit the cows. *Nangabot ang 2 ka nga apo sa Hillsbrook Farm, mamisita kuno sila sa mga baka.* They could not stop smiling. *Malipayon kaayo!*

Hillsbrook is a family farm, and that means the next generation grows up around the herd: learning where milk comes from, how to be gentle with animals, and how much care goes into every bottle.
