Clean milk starts long before the bottle. In May 2026, **53 farmers from Socorro** joined a training on **hygienic milk production**, part of the National Dairy Authority's **Dairy Herd Build-Up Program**.

The farmers learned how to keep the cow, the hands and the equipment clean at milking time, and how to keep fresh milk cold and safe until it reaches the customer.

Thank you to the **DOST** for the snacks and lunch, and to every farmer who came. A strong dairy community on the island is good for all of us.
