On race day of the **Bucas Grande Half Marathon**, Hillsbrook Farm ran a food station at the **Socorro Town Plaza, near the Municipal Kiosk**.

Runners and their families tried samples of our **Island Pure fresh milk**, our **chocolate milk** and our **ube milk**. Milk is a great drink after a long run: it has water, protein and natural sugars to help the body recover.

Thank you to the organisers and to every runner who stopped by. See you at the next race.
